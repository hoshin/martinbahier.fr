Welcome! This space is where I publish short notes, learnings and thoughts — hopefully on both on the **engineering** and the **Overwatch casting** sides.

## Why a blog

Hopefully, this'll be a more permanent spot for me than pure microblogging. Also, I'm not sure what I'll have there would really fit on other platforms since there'll likely hardly be any direction to it.

## How it works

Each post is just a Markdown file in the site's repository. No CMS, no tracking — just text and a few links.

Each article, unless very specific to a language, is likely to get its EN _and_ FR version.

See you around!
